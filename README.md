# Rin Chat

Rin Chat is a free desktop app for AI roleplay with character cards, made by Rin Chat LLC. Use any provider with
your own API key, or a local model; your chats stay encrypted on your machine. Windows and Linux.

## Bugs, questions and feature ideas

- **Bugs:** [open an issue](https://github.com/Rin-Chat-LLC/rin-chat-docs/issues/new/choose). Search the
  [existing issues](https://github.com/Rin-Chat-LLC/rin-chat-docs/issues) first; a thumbs-up on a match helps more
  than a duplicate.
- **Questions:** ask in [Help](https://github.com/Rin-Chat-LLC/rin-chat-docs/discussions/categories/help), where
  answers stay searchable.
- **Feature ideas:** post or upvote in
  [Feature Ideas](https://github.com/Rin-Chat-LLC/rin-chat-docs/discussions/categories/feature-ideas).
- **Characters, lorebooks and models:** share and compare in
  [Character Cards and Lorebooks](https://github.com/Rin-Chat-LLC/rin-chat-docs/discussions/categories/character-cards-and-lorebooks)
  and [Models and Presets](https://github.com/Rin-Chat-LLC/rin-chat-docs/discussions/categories/models-and-presets),
  or on [r/Rinchat](https://www.reddit.com/r/Rinchat/).
- **Prefer Discord?** Post in [#bug-reports](https://discord.gg/KrAsdQ8x9t) or
  [#feature-requests](https://discord.gg/CvnnDnq7tC); each link also joins you to the server.
- For security problems, do **not** open a public issue. Email info@rin.chat or
  [DM me on Discord](https://discord.com/users/1385702791766610032).
